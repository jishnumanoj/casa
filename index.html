Questions, answered

A few things you may be wondering.

Have another question? Let's talk.

How do we get started?

Share a little about your project through the enquiry form. We’ll get in touch to understand your needs and discuss a first conversation.

What does a project cost?

Every project is different. Costs depend on the scope, site and selections. Tell us what you have in mind and we can discuss an initial estimate.

How long does a project take?

Timelines vary with project size, approvals and site conditions. We’ll outline a realistic schedule once we understand your brief.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Kerala. Please share your project location and we’ll confirm how we can help.

Can you work with my architect or plans?

We’re happy to discuss working with your existing plans or architect. Share the details and we can explore the best way forward.
